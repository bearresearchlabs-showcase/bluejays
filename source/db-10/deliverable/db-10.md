## Query 1: Can you show me a multi-retailer price comparison that includes geographic filtering and temporal trend analysis? {#query-1}

**Use Case:** **Can you show me a multi-retailer price comparison that includes geographic filtering and temporal trend analysis?**

**Description:** The pricing analytics team needs to monitor competitive pricing across multiple retailers and regions to inform pricing strategy decisions. Retailers are adjusting prices dynamically, and the business needs visibility into which competitors offer the best prices in specific markets and how these prices trend over time. Generate a product pricing comparison report that identifies the best prices by retailer and location, reveals price trends over time, and calculates competitive positioning metrics. The query joins product, retailer, and location tables, groups by product, retailer, and geographic dimensions, computes price aggregates (min, max, avg) and quartiles for competitive positioning, applies window functions to calculate rolling averages and period-over-period price changes, filters by specified date ranges and geographic boundaries, and handles NULL values in optional location fields. The output returns a dataset with product-level pricing by r

**Business Value:** Product pricing comparison report showing best prices by retailer and location, price trends, and competitive positioning metrics

**Complexity:** moderate

            ORDER BY gmm.price_effective_date

    FROM retailer_market_positioning rmp
    INNER JOIN geographic_market_mapping gmm ON rmp.product_id = gmm.product_id

        AND rmp.market_id = gmm.market_id
),

        tpt.retailer_id,
        tpt.retailer_name,
        tpt.retailer_type,
        tpt.retailer_price,
        tpt.price_position,
        tpt.price_rank,
        tpt.price_difference_from_min,
        ROUND(CAST(tpt.price_premium_percentage AS NUMERIC), 2) AS price_premium_percentage,
        tpt.retailer_price_type,
        tpt.retailer_discount,
        tpt.retailer_count,
        tpt.store_count,
        tpt.physical_store_count,
        tpt.online_retailer_count,
        tpt.min_price,
        tpt.max_price,
        ROUND(CAST(tpt.avg_price AS NUMERIC), 2) AS avg_price,
        ROUND(CAST(tpt.median_price AS NUMERIC), 2) AS median_price,
        ROUND(CAST(tpt.price_std_dev AS NUMERIC), 2) AS price_std_dev,

        CASE
            WHEN tpt.prev_price IS NOT NULL THEN tpt.retailer_price - tpt.prev_price
            ELSE NULL
        END AS price_change,
        CASE
            WHEN tpt.prev_price IS NOT NULL AND tpt.prev_price != 0 THEN
                ((tpt.retailer_price - tpt.prev_price) / tpt.prev_price) * 100
            ELSE NULL
        END AS price_change_percentage,
        ROUND(CAST(tpt.moving_avg_price_7d AS NUMERIC), 2) AS moving_avg_price_7d,

## Query 2: Can you show me inventory availability predictions based on historical patterns along with geographic distribution? {#query-2}

WITH RECURSIVE inventory_history AS (

        pi.inventory_id,

    ROUND(CAST(prediction_confidence AS NUMERIC), 2) AS prediction_confidence,
    ROUND(CAST(avg_stock_30d AS NUMERIC), 0) AS avg_stock_30d,
    ROUND(CAST(stock_volatility_30d AS NUMERIC), 2) AS stock_volatility_30d,
    avg_stock_by_dow,

    avg_stock_by_state
